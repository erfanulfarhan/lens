Every AI assistant that can read your screen wants a monthly fee and a copy of everything it sees.

I built one that wants neither.

Lens looks at your screen, follows your calls, and answers from your own documents, with the model running on your own computer. No subscription. Nothing uploaded.

It even checks your hardware and picks a model that will actually run on it.

Free and open source, for Mac and Windows:
https://github.com/erfanulfarhan/lens

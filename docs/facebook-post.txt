I built my own AI assistant, and it runs entirely on my computer.

Most AI assistants that can see your screen cost a monthly subscription, and every
screenshot goes to a company's servers. I wanted to know whether I could build the
same thing the other way round: your machine, your model, your data.

So I built Lens.

It sees your screen when you ask it to, listens to your calls and transcribes them
on the device, and answers using your own documents. The model runs locally, so
there is no subscription and nothing leaves your computer. If you already own an
API key it works with that too.

A few things I am proud of:

- It reads your RAM and graphics card and recommends a model that will actually run
  smoothly, instead of leaving you to guess between a dozen names
- You can host the model on a different computer on your network, so a laptop stays
  cool and quiet while a desktop does the heavy lifting
- It reads your PDFs and notes and pulls only the relevant parts into each answer,
  so a large library still works on a small model
- The whole thing is open source

Free, private, and yours. Installers for macOS and Windows are on GitHub:

https://github.com/erfanulfarhan/lens

Would genuinely love feedback if you try it.
